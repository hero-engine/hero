UX exploration for the Hero QA domain pack — the quality surface that lives alongside Hero's engineering and PM surfaces in the same workspace. Thesis: Hero QA is the quality spine of the sprint and release, not a TestRail clone with chat. Coverage is a story-completion gate; regression status is a release-readiness signal; AI authors 40-80 test cases per sprint in minutes; defects from TestRail/Xray/Jira/GitHub are unified into one queue by normalization (kept fidelity, no migration).

AI-assisted workflow is the centerpiece, not decoration. Every screen renders the four AI surfaces that make Hero feel like a colleague, not a tool: a prominent Chat entry pinned at the top of the left nav (bolt icon); the inline-propose pattern where agents like test-author and qa-investigator land proposed content in the artifact (case rows, finding text, verdict reasoning) with per-item Accept/Edit/Reject and "⏎ to accept" hints; a right-rail chat with a sticky ambient region atop a rolling agent conversation showing real prompts, agent responses, and system events; a two-row bottom strip combining state-aware verb buttons with a chat input carrying the model chip (Sonnet 4.6), / and @ tool affordances. Non-engineering domains welcome heavier AI assistance — these surfaces ship leaned-in, not hidden.

What's QA-unique beyond the shared AI grammar: coverage signals in story rows, AC-traceable case rendering with derivation provenance, the three-action rejection composer on stories in qa-ready (Add AC · Suggest new story · Reject as quality issue — preventing scope-creep through the gate), the four-action test-issue triage (bad test · reject story · raise bug · flag regression) ranked by `test-issue-triager` confidence, the active flaky backlog with verdict workflow driving toward zero, and the release-gate verdict banner aggregating coverage + regression + blockers into Go/No-Go with the reasoning chain visible.

The Reject (compose findings) button on a story in qa-ready is the platform thesis in one click — the QA equivalent of PM's Hand off to /design. It lives inline-prominent in the bottom strip with all three radio actions equally visible, and the findings textarea is an AI-proposed block from qa-investigator, not a blank field. Cyan-teal (#0891b2) is reserved for QA-authored cross-pack content (the collapsible QA Findings block written into PM stories on rejection).

Nine screens follow. Open them in order: Story queue (default landing) → Story qa-ready rejection (brand interaction) → Test plan editor with proposed Case-C6 → Test case editor (AI-authored provenance + history) → Unified inbox (ranked-confidence triage) → Release-gate (reasoned Go/No-Go) → Flaky backlog (proposed verdicts) → Cross-domain handoff stream (pattern detection) → AI authoring mid-flight (the brand demo — five proposed cases landing inline in real time).

Local-first with seamless hybrid escalation. Unified inbox works without integration; TestRail / Xray / Jira / GitHub are additive decoration, never a prerequisite. spec L3 / L28
