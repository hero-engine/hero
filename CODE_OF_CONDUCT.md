# Contributor Code of Conduct

Hero's community should be a place where people can collaborate without
harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Make room for different experience levels and communication styles.
- Protect private information and follow coordinated-disclosure requests.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's
private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces represented as part of the Hero project. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation
when behavior violates this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately through the route in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), identifying the report as a conduct matter. Do not
publish sensitive conduct reports in an issue. Before public launch, authorized
collaborators should use their existing private GitHub channel with the
repository owner; the anonymous private route becomes available only after the
security setting and visibility gates complete.
