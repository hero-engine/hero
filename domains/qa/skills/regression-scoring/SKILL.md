# Regression scoring

Score stability, blast radius, customer impact, recurrence likelihood, uniqueness,
and execution cost with visible inputs. Promote cases that protect valuable,
repeatable behavior at sustainable cost. Penalize redundant cases and cases whose
signal is not trustworthy. A high-impact but unstable case should trigger repair,
not silent exclusion. Record why promoted cases belong in the suite so later
curation can distinguish durable protection from historical inertia.
