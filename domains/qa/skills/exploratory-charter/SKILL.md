# Exploratory charter

Write the mission as "Explore <target> with <resources or heuristics> to discover
<risk or information>." Include setup, data, constraints, a timebox, useful tours
or heuristics, evidence capture, and stop conditions. During execution distinguish
observations, questions, issues, coverage, and follow-up ideas. Debrief what was
covered, what surprised the tester, what remains uncertain, and which findings
deserve durable artifacts.
