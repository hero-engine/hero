# Context-driven principles

Start from the product mission, users, failure costs, team skills, technology,
schedule, and available evidence. Practices have value only in context, so explain
why a technique fits this situation and what it costs. Prefer skilled judgment and
continuous learning over ritual compliance. When a preset conflicts with observed
risk, surface the mismatch and propose an explicit adjustment rather than quietly
ignoring either one.
