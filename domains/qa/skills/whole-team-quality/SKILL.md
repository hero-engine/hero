# Whole-team quality

QA supplies testing craft, risk visibility, and independent challenge; it does not
become the only owner of quality. Put prevention and fast feedback near design and
implementation, preserve a clear path for QA findings, and avoid late-stage ticket
throwing. A rejection must distinguish implementation failure from missing
requirements or new scope. A seam request must explain the testing need while
leaving engineering ownership intact.
