# Coverage budgeting

Inventory criteria and risks, then estimate authoring, setup, execution,
investigation, and maintenance cost. Allocate deeper coverage to high-impact,
high-change, or hard-to-detect behavior. Preserve a small budget for exploration
and regression maintenance. Report the proposed case count only alongside the
behavior it covers. When capacity is insufficient, name the deferred behavior,
residual risk, and evidence that would justify changing the allocation.
