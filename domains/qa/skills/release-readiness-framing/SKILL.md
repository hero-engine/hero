# Release readiness framing

State the release candidate and evidence timestamp. Summarize committed behavior,
critical-risk coverage, regression state, unresolved failures, environment gaps,
observability, rollback readiness, and approved waivers. Apply the configured
blocker policy consistently and show the calculation. Separate facts from the Go
or No-Go recommendation. Missing evidence lowers confidence and may itself be a
blocker when the affected risk is critical.
