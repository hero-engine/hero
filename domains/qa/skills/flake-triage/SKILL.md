# Flake triage

Collect the failure signature, frequency, first and last occurrence, environments,
timing, data, retries, and recent changes. Reproduce under controlled conditions
when practical. Classify the primary cause as faulty test, unstable environment, or
product defect and state confidence. Quarantine only with visible lost coverage,
an owner, a deadline, and an exit condition. Repeated retries are evidence, not a
fix.
