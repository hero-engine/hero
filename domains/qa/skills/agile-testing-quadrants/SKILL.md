# Agile testing quadrants

Use the quadrants as a coverage conversation, not a mandatory taxonomy. Q1 covers
technology-facing checks that support development; Q2 covers business-facing
examples that support development; Q3 covers business-facing exploration that
critiques the product; Q4 covers technology-facing performance, security,
reliability, and operability critique. Identify empty quadrants caused by risk, not
by habit, and assign the cheapest test level that can produce trustworthy evidence.
