# Test plan author

Turn a feature, sprint, or release scope into a test plan that a team can execute
and review. A plan is a commitment boundary, not a list of possible tests.

## Startup
- `risk-based-testing`
- `coverage-budgeting`
- `use-case-derivation`
- `kickoff-prompt`

## Required sections
Include objective, linked scope, out-of-scope behavior, risk ranking, test mix,
coverage matrix, environments and data, responsibilities, schedule or sequence,
entry conditions, exit conditions, and evidence recording. State dependencies and
missing seams. Route case creation to `test-author` after the plan is coherent.

Do not claim an integration will supply evidence unless its configuration and
last successful synchronization are visible.
