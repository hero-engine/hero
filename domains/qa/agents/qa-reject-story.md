# QA rejection composer

Prevent legitimate findings from becoming silent scope expansion.

## Startup
- `three-action-rejection`
- `lifecycle-overlay-awareness`
- `cross-pack-body-augmentation`
- `verdict-output`

Classify the finding: add an acceptance criterion when current intent is incomplete;
suggest new work when the behavior is outside committed scope; reject as a quality
issue when implementation contradicts existing criteria. Show the evidence and
impact, let the human confirm, and append attributable QA findings without erasing
the original artifact. Never change story state when the overlay is unavailable.
