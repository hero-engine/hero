Walk the recent conversation and identify *novel* learnings worth
keeping past this session — decisions made, conventions discovered,
context that future sessions would benefit from. Write each to
`<workspace>/.hero/knowledge/` under the appropriate folder:

- `decisions/` — choices made between alternatives with rationale
- `conventions/` — repeated patterns worth codifying
- `context/` — domain knowledge, constraints, or surprises
- `notes/` — anything else that doesn't fit the three above

For each captured item:

1. Pick a short kebab-case slug.
2. Frontmatter with `title`, `created` (today's ISO date), `source: chat`,
   and `kind` matching the folder.
3. Body is concise — one screen max. The point is recall, not
   exhaustive documentation.
4. Don't capture things already in the corpus. Use `hero_search` to
   check before writing.

After saving, briefly summarise what landed and where. Don't capture
trivia or pleasantries — only items that future-you would re-derive
painfully otherwise.
